01 — Founder · Shipped SaaS · Full-stack

BizFalcon AI — AI Analytics Company, Founded & Shipped Solo

A real SaaS company I founded and built end-to-end — model, interface, billing, deployment. The flagship, Talon, is an AI data analyst that writes and runs Python on your full dataset in the browser: every number computed, never estimated, with the formula behind each figure one click away. The same engine ships as ProfitFalcon (e-commerce unit economics — per-SKU margins, discount-leak detection, reorder forecasting) and ClientFalcon (agency client reporting — prospect audits, portfolio health, branded reports), plus Fledge for creator analytics. On top sits an operator layer that recommends the exact next move — cut this budget, reorder this SKU — waits for human approval, then scores every recommendation against what actually happened in a decision ledger. OAuth connectors (Meta, Google Ads, GA4, Shopify, Klaviyo), Stripe subscriptions, 9 industry metric packs, 400+ automated tests + CI.
